VidQuix Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Last updated: 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VidQuix (the “App”), published by India Apps (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use the Android app com.indiaapps.vidquix. VidQuix lets you download publicly available videos and audio from supported websites to your own device.

In short:

  • VidQuix has no accounts, no sign-in, and we do not run a server that collects your data.
  • The links you paste, your download history and your settings stay on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location, photos, or the contents of your files. We do not use cookies or website log-ins; VidQuix only works with public content and does not access your accounts on other websites.

2. Information handled on your device

The following is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Links you enter or share to the App, and the video information fetched for them (title, thumbnail, available qualities).
  • Download history and queue, kept in a local database so you can see, replay or delete your downloads.
  • Settings, such as theme, default quality and an optional proxy address you enter.
  • Downloaded media files, saved to the Download/VidQuix folder on your device.
  • Diagnostic logs kept locally for troubleshooting. Credentials, cookies and proxy passwords are redacted from them. They leave your device only if you choose to send them to us, for example by email.

You can remove this data at any time by deleting items in the App, clearing the App’s storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the App.

3. Network connections the App makes

To work, the App connects to the internet. Doing so means the following third parties receive technical data such as your IP address and device/network information, under their own policies:

  • The websites you download from. When you paste a link, the App contacts that website (using the open-source tool yt-dlp) to read the video details and download the file. That website sees your IP address and the request, just as if you had opened the link in a browser.
  • Your proxy, if you set one. If you enter a proxy in Settings, requests go through that proxy server, which is operated by someone else.
  • GitHub. The App periodically downloads a small configuration file (ad frequency rules and in-app prompts) from a public GitHub repository. GitHub receives your IP address as part of that request. No personal data is sent in the request.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

VidQuix is supported by ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). AdMob may collect and use, among other things, your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system information, app interaction and ad performance data, and approximate location derived from your IP address, to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and (where permitted) personalise ads.

  • In regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK and some US states) the App shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) and respects your choice. You can change your choice from the privacy options in the App’s Settings when available.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Learn more: How Google uses data from ads and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Google Play services

The App may use the Google Play In-App Review API to ask whether you would like to rate the App. Google handles that interaction under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive your review data through it.

7. Backups

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android or Google may back up some of the App’s settings and data to your Google account, subject to Google’s terms. This is controlled by your device settings.

8. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described above (Google AdMob and Google Play services, the websites you choose to download from, your proxy, and GitHub) in the ways described in this policy, or if required by law.

9. Data retention and your choices

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. Locally stored data stays until you delete it or uninstall the App. For data held by Google (ads), use your Google account controls. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data; to exercise them for data held by third parties, contact that third party, or contact us and we will help where we can.

10. Children’s privacy

VidQuix is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help.

11. Security

Connections to remote services use HTTPS where the service supports it. Your downloads and history remain in the App’s storage and your Download folder on your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

12. Responsible use

VidQuix is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any video website. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to download and use any content, and for following the terms of the websites and the copyright laws that apply to you.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be available at this page with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes will be reflected in the App or on its Google Play listing.
